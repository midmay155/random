Privacy Policy — Random

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · applies to Random version 2.0.0 and later

Random we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, and the choices and rights you have — including additional information for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain U.S. states.

1. Who We Are

Random is developed and published by an individual developer:

  • Developer (data controller): Mid-May
  • Country: Vietnam
  • Email: midmay155@gmail.com

2. Information We Process

The app does not require account registration or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or email inside the app.

2.1 Data Stored Only on Your Device

Content you create while using the app — Lucky Spin themes and option lists, result history, card decks, language, sound and haptic settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

2.2 Photo Library Access

The Random Card feature lets you select images from your device's photo library to create draw cards. Selected images are used only locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server. You can revoke photo access at any time in your device's system settings.

2.3 Usage and Crash Data (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics — app usage events (e.g. which features are opened), an app-instance identifier, and basic device/OS information, used to understand and improve the app. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, advertising-related analytics signals are off by default and follow the choices you make in the consent message (see Section 4).
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash logs (device model, OS version, app state at the time of the crash) used to detect and fix errors.
  • Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Messaging — an app-instance identifier used to deliver app configuration and, if you allow notifications, update notifications.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded, app open) from Google AdMob so the app can stay free. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (or, where unavailable, other device identifiers);
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language) and ad interactions.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized / limited depending on your region and your choices.

2.5 Subscription (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, No Ads, that removes ads. If you subscribe:

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number, bank details or other payment information.
  • The app uses Google Play Billing to receive only what is needed to provide the subscription: the subscription plan you chose (weekly, monthly or yearly), the purchase time and whether the subscription is active and set to renew. It does not include your name, email or payment details.
  • This status is stored on your device so that ads stay off, and it is re-checked with Google Play each time the app is opened. If the subscription ends or is refunded, ads are shown again.
  • We do not use this information to identify you.

4. Your Privacy Choices

  • Consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland). The first time you open the app in these regions, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform) asks for your choices. Ads are only requested after you have made a choice. If you do not consent, you may still see limited ads that do not use personal data for personalization.
  • Change or withdraw your choices at any time. Tap the Privacy options button (🛡 shield icon) on the app's Home screen to reopen the privacy form. This button is shown wherever privacy options are required.
  • U.S. state privacy rights. If you live in a U.S. state with a consumer privacy law (e.g. California), use the same Privacy options button to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising.
  • Device settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (e.g. Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on the device).
  • Notifications. You can allow or deny notification permission at any time in your device settings.

5. Who Receives Data

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited — as provider of Firebase and AdMob. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play (Google Play Billing) — processes your subscription payment and tells the app whether your subscription is active. See Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising partners listed in the consent message, only according to your choices.

We do not sell your personal data for money. However, allowing ad partners to use data for personalized advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under some U.S. state laws; you can opt out as described in Section 4.

6. International Transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own. For transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Subscription, Cancellation and Refunds

  • The No Ads subscription renews automatically at the end of each period (weekly, monthly or yearly, depending on the plan) until you cancel. The price and billing period are shown in the app and in Google Play before you confirm the purchase.
  • You can cancel or manage your subscription at any time in Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. After you cancel, you keep ad-free access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Deleting or uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription; you must cancel it in Google Play.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play in line with its refund policy; we cannot process payments or refunds directly.
  • If you reinstall the app or use a new device with the same Google account, use Restore purchase in the app to bring back your subscription.

8. How Long Data Is Kept

  • Data stored on your device, including the subscription status, stays there until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Your purchase record is kept by Google Play according to Google's policies.
  • Analytics, crash and advertising data processed by Google are kept for the periods described in Google's documentation and our Firebase/Google Analytics retention settings, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

9. Your Rights

EEA, UK and Switzerland. You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

U.S. states with privacy laws. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know/access, correct and delete personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to exercise your rights. Use the Privacy options button in the app, your device settings, or email us at midmay155@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we do not hold data that directly identifies you; for data held by Google, we will help you as far as we can, and you can also use Google's own tools. We respond within the time required by applicable law (for example, one month under GDPR, 45 days under California law).

Deleting local data. Clear the app's data via Settings › Apps › Random › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.
