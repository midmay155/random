Privacy Policy — Random

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Random ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require account registration or sign-in, and does not directly collect personally identifiable information (name, phone number, email, etc.).

1.1 Data Stored on Your Device

Content you create while using the app — Lucky Spin themes and option lists, result history, language settings, sound settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

1.2 Photo Library Access

The Random Card feature lets you select images from your device's photo library to create draw cards. Selected images are used only locally on your device for display within the app and are never uploaded to any server. You can revoke photo access permission at any time in your device's system settings.

1.3 Data Automatically Collected via Third Parties

The app uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage data (in-app events, feature usage frequency, basic device/OS information) to help us improve the app experience.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash logs when the app encounters an error, including device information, OS version, and app state at the time of the crash, to help us detect and fix issues.

This data is processed by Google LLC in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not independently collect or store this data on our own systems.

2. How We Use Data

The data described above is used only to:

  • Operate the app's core features
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix bugs/crashes
  • Analyze anonymous usage statistics to improve the product

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

3. Your Rights

Since app data (themes, lists, history) is stored locally, you can delete all of it at any time by:

  • Clearing app data via Settings > Apps > Random > Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the app

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data within the scope the app manages. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
